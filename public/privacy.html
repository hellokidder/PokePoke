Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

PokePoke ("we", "us", or "the app") is a macOS menu-bar utility that notifies you when your local coding agents need attention. This policy explains what data the app and this website handle.

1. The app is local-first

PokePoke runs entirely on your Mac. All session data it tracks — project names, session status, and notification history — is stored locally under ~/.pokepoke/ on your own machine. The app communicates with your coding agents over a local Unix socket.

  • We do not operate any server that receives your usage data.
  • We do not collect, transmit, or store your prompts, code, logs, or terminal output.
  • The app requires no account and no sign-in.

2. No network calls

PokePoke makes no outbound network requests at all. There is no license check, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no update ping from the app itself (the optional in-app updater contacts GitHub Releases only when you click "Check for updates").

3. No payments

PokePoke is free. No payment information is collected, processed, or stored — by us or by any third party. If a voluntary donation option is added later, this policy will be updated before it goes live.

4. This website

This site is a static marketing and download page. It does not require sign-in, loads no third-party scripts, and uses no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Standard server logs may be retained by our hosting provider for security and reliability.

5. Your rights

Because the app stores data only on your device, you can delete all of it at any time by removing the ~/.pokepoke/ directory and uninstalling the app. There is no server-side data about you to request or delete.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hellokiddermail@gmail.com.

隐私政策

最后更新:2026 年 11 月 11 日

PokePoke(下称“我们”或“本应用”)是一款 macOS 菜单栏工具,在你本地的 coding agent 需要你时发出提醒。本政策说明本应用与本网站如何处理数据。

1. 本应用本地优先

PokePoke 完全在你的 Mac 上运行。它追踪的所有会话数据——项目名、会话状态、提醒记录—— 都只存放在你本机的 ~/.pokepoke/ 目录下。应用通过本地 Unix socket 与你的 coding agent 通信。

  • 我们不运营任何接收你使用数据的服务器。
  • 我们不收集、传输或存储你的 prompt、代码、日志或终端输出。
  • 本应用无需注册、无需登录。

2. 没有任何网络请求

PokePoke 完全不做任何对外网络请求:没有授权校验、没有遥测、没有崩溃上报; 应用自身也不会主动检查更新(可选的更新器只在你点击「检查更新」时访问 GitHub Releases)。

3. 没有支付

PokePoke 是免费的:我们不收集、不处理、不存储任何支付信息,也没有任何第三方经手。 若将来开放自愿捐赠,本政策会在上线前更新。

4. 关于本网站

本网站是一个静态的介绍与下载页面:无需登录、不加载任何第三方脚本、不使用广告或跨站追踪 Cookie。托管服务商可能出于安全与稳定性保留标准的服务器日志。

5. 你的权利

由于本应用仅在你的设备上存储数据,你可随时删除 ~/.pokepoke/ 目录并卸载应用以 清除全部数据。服务端没有任何关于你的数据可供索取或删除。

6. 联系方式

对本政策有疑问?请邮件联系 hellokiddermail@gmail.com。
