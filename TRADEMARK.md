# 商标与品牌使用

PokePoke 的**源代码**以 [MIT](LICENSE) 授权，你可以自由使用、修改、再分发，包括商业用途。

**这个名字和图标不在授权范围内。**

MIT 只授予版权许可，不授予商标权。以下用法需要事先获得作者书面许可：

- 用 `PokePoke` / `Poke Poke` 作为你的 fork、衍生作品、发行版或产品的名字
- 使用项目图标（八爪鱼形象 `src-tauri/icons/*`）作为你的应用图标或品牌标识
- 以任何方式暗示你的版本是官方版本、或获得官方背书

**允许的用法：**

- 在文档、文章、评测里指名提到 PokePoke（nominative use）
- 说明「本项目基于 PokePoke」并附上原仓库链接
- 原样分发官方构建产物（不得修改后再以官方名义分发）

如果你发布修改版，请换一个名字，并在 README 里说明它与本项目的区别。这样用户不会混淆谁该为哪个版本负责——这既是法律要求，也是对使用者的基本保护。

# Trademark & brand usage

PokePoke's **source code** is licensed under [MIT](LICENSE) — use it, modify it, redistribute it, including commercially.

**The name and the logo are not part of that grant.**

MIT grants copyright permissions only, not trademark rights. Prior written permission is required to:

- Use `PokePoke` / `Poke Poke` as the name of your fork, derivative, distribution, or product
- Use the project icon (the octopus artwork in `src-tauri/icons/*`) as your app icon or brand mark
- Imply in any way that your build is official or endorsed

**Always fine:**

- Referring to PokePoke by name in docs, articles, or reviews (nominative use)
- Stating "based on PokePoke" with a link back to the original repo
- Redistributing official builds unmodified (never rebranded as official)

If you ship a modified build, pick a different name and make the difference clear in your README. Users deserve to know who is responsible for the build they are running.
